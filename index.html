A job post is not a client.

Lists of open vacancies tell you who is hiring. They don’t tell you which of those companies might pay an agency, what the search really needs, or what to say in the first message.

So your team spends business development hours qualifying accounts that were never a fit, and sends outreach that says nothing specific.

RecruitCairn does the qualifying research first and shows its work: what the sources say, what we think it means, and what we could not confirm.

How each opportunity is researched

Signals are cheap. The work is deciding which ones point to a real conversation for your desk.

  1. We scan public hiring signals

    Employer job boards and careers pages, filtered to the roles and US markets your agency serves. On October 21, 2026, one scan read 1,562 public job posts from 36 company job boards; 55 posts from 30 companies passed the first filters.

  2. We research and challenge each account

    We check the source, the date, location limits and any statement against agencies, then look for context: several roles at once, new teams, recent funding. Evidence and interpretation are kept apart.

  3. You get a brief your team can act on

    A prioritised opportunity with sources, the commercial case, what is still unknown and a suggested first approach. Every brief is dated and reviewed before delivery.

What every brief contains

Enough to decide in a few minutes whether an account deserves a call, and to open that call with something relevant.

Company
Name, official website, sector and location.
Hiring signal

First approach

Questions agencies ask

How is this different from a list of job openings?

A list stops at “this company is hiring”. Each brief adds the evidence with sources and dates, why the account may be worth approaching for your specialty, what we could not confirm, a priority and a suggested first approach.

Does a hiring signal mean the company wants an agency?

No. A public vacancy shows hiring activity, not willingness to pay a recruiting agency. We keep the evidence, our interpretation and the open questions separate, and we flag any statement against agency approaches.

Will this guarantee new clients or meetings?

No. We provide researched account intelligence and a suggested approach. Booking meetings, winning mandates and making placements stay with your team.

Which agencies is it best for?

Specialist and boutique agencies that win clients through their own business development: technology and software, data and AI, sales and go-to-market, marketing, finance and executive search, recruiting for employers in the US.

Are contacts included?

When a relevant professional contact or channel can be legitimately sourced and verified, yes. Some briefs won’t include a named contact. We never guess email addresses.

Send the form below with your specialties and markets. We reply by email to confirm fit, timing and accounts to exclude, then send the five briefs as a document with source links.

What does the pilot include?

Ten researched opportunities over 14 days, in two deliveries of five, tailored to your roles and markets, for USD 299. We agree dates and scope before you pay, and it does not renew automatically.

Is the research exclusive to my agency?

Not by default. We tailor the selection to your desk, but another agency could research the same employer. Exclusivity would need a separate written agreement.
